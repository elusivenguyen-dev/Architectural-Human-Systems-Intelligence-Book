### 12.1 Stress Overload Architecture

**Định nghĩa:** "Stress overload is a systemic cascade in which accumulated allostatic load exceeds the Human OS capacity for regulation, recovery, and adaptation."

**8-Phase Cascade:**
```
Phase 1: Load Accumulation     → Stress × Time × Uncertainty > Resistance
Phase 2: Regulation Depletion  → L2 + L3 + L7 lose coordination
Phase 3: Executive Degradation → Prefrontal downregulation, amygdala dominance
Phase 4: Model Rigidity        → Black-and-white thinking, predictive accuracy loss
Phase 5: Identity Defense      → Defensive ego activation, blame externalization
Phase 6: Meaning Collapse      → Purpose fragmentation, existential fatigue
Phase 7: Social Disintegration → Trust erosion, coalition breakdown, memetic toxicity
Phase 8: System Collapse       → Resistance → 0; Qi fragmented across all layers
```

**Key Insight:** "Stress overload is not solved by removing stressors. It is solved by restoring cross-layer coherence faster than load accumulates."

**Protocol 8 bước:**
1. STOP LOAD — Không quyết định quan trọng khi prefrontal offline
2. RESTORE SUBSTRATE — Sleep first, glucose stability, grounding
3. REGULATE NERVOUS SYSTEM — HRV training, coherent breathing (5-6 breaths/min)
4. REPAIR MODELS — Delay major decisions 48-72h, externalize qua writing
5. DECOUPLE EGO — "I am not my stress response"
6. RECONSTRUCT MEANING — Reconnect load to purpose
7. RE-SYNCHRONIZE FIELDS — One-on-one trust repair before group alignment
8. ADAPTIVE RETURN — Re-enter at 60% capacity, update Resistance equation

### 12.2 Greed Architecture

**Định nghĩa:** "Greed is a systemic feedback loop in which the self-model (L4) becomes fused with resource-acquisition metrics, bypassing meaning coherence (L5) and reality constraints (L6)."

**Greed Stack:**
```
L4 Self-Model: "I am my net worth / power / accumulation"
    ↓
L13 Status/Incentive: Systems reward acquisition → dopaminergic reinforcement
    ↓
L3 Mental Models: Scale blindness — "more is always better"
    ↓
L5 Spirit: Meaning collapses into "having"
    ↓
L6 Physics: Reality constraints ignored — entropy, limits denied
    ↓
L2 Emotion: Anxiety masked by accumulation
    ↓
F-1 Social Field: Coalition weaponized for extraction
```

**Protocol Deconstruction:**
1. Separate Self from Metric
2. Restore Nonlinear Vision — "đủ" là một số có thể định nghĩa
3. Rebuild Meaning Independent of Acquisition
4. Repair Social Field — extraction → contribution
5. Entropy Accounting — tính chi phí duy trì

### 12.3 Anger Architecture

**Định nghĩa:** "Anger is a biological-social defense mechanism that, when unregulated, becomes a structural weapon deployed by the defensive ego (L6) to protect self-model integrity."

**Anger Stack:**
```
L6 Defensive Egoic: Identity threat detected
    ↓
L2 Emotion: Sympathetic surge → adrenaline/cortisol
    ↓
L1 Brain: Amygdala hijack + prefrontal suppression
    ↓
L3 Mental Models: "They are wrong / unfair / attacking"
    ↓
L4 Self-Model: Boundary violation = ego survival threat
    ↓
L13 Status/Conflict: Anger as status enforcement
    ↓
F-3 EM Field: Cardiac coherence collapse
    ↓
F-1 Social Field: Emotional contagion — anger spreads, polarizes
```

**Protocol Regulation & Integration:**
1. **Detect Before Deploy:** Interoception — nhận biết nhiệt, co thắt, nhịp tim
2. **Separate Threat from Violation:** "Nguy hiểm thực sự hay ego bị xâm phạm?"
3. **Restore Prefrontal Online:** 6 breaths, 90 giây — không quyết định, không nói
4. **Channel Boundary Without Destruction:** Anger = thông tin về boundary
5. **Integrate Underlying Fear:** "Giận này che giấu nỗi sợ gì?"
6. **Repair Field Damage:** Xin lỗi không phải vì cảm xúc, mà vì cách dùng nó làm vũ khí
