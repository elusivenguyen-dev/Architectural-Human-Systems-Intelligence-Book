### M01-M10: Nền Tảng

**M01: Human OS begins at the field, not the neuron.**  
*Layer:* L0 | *Field:* F-5 | *Module:* Meta-Awareness  
> Ý thức không được sản xuất bởi não; nó là một hiện tượng trường mà não bộ localize và integrate.

**M02: The body is the foundation of the conscious mind.**  
*Layer:* L1/L2 | *Field:* F-2/F-3 | *Module:* Executive Psychology  
> Não phục vụ cơ thể, không phải ngược lại. Protoself là nền tảng của mọi self-awareness.

**M03: Interoception is the basis of emotional awareness.**  
*Layer:* L2 | *Field:* F-3 | *Module:* Fear Systems  
> Mọi cảm xúc đều bắt đầu từ cảm giác cơ thể. Anterior insula là "interoceptive cortex."

**M04: Prediction error drives all learning.**  
*Layer:* L3 | *Field:* F-4 | *Module:* Human Adaptation Loops  
> Não bộ là một prediction machine. Free energy = prediction error to be minimized.

**M05: The self-model is a controlled hallucination.**  
*Layer:* L4 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Organizational Identity  
> Self không phải là một thực thể; nó là một model được brain generate để predict và control.

**M06: Meaning is the coherence field of Spirit layer.**  
*Layer:* L5 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Narrative Control  
> Purpose không phải là một đích đến; nó là một trường coherence giữa các layer.

**M07: Physics constraints are non-negotiable.**  
*Layer:* L6 | *Field:* F-5 | *Module:* Elite Cognition  
> Entropy, limits, tradeoffs không thể bị deny. Reality constraints always win.

**M08: Resistance = (Energy × Recovery × Meaning) / (Load × Time × Uncertainty).**  
*Layer:* L8 | *Field:* F-2/F-3 | *Module:* Burnout Entropy  
> Resistance không phải là một trait; nó là một dynamic equation.

**M09: Understanding people requires understanding their attachment history.**  
*Layer:* L4 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Hiểu Nhân  
> Internal working models từ thời thơ ấu shape tất cả adult relationships.

**M10: The environment is not a backdrop; it is a co-author.**  
*Layer:* L10 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Thuận Địa  
> Thuận Địa = reading the field of the environment, not just the objects in it.

### M11-M20: Hệ Thống

**M11: Motivation is incentive stack alignment, not willpower.**  
*Layer:* L4/L13 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Motivation Architecture  
> Willpower là một illusion. Motivation = stack of incentives aligned across layers.

**M12: Trust is the bandwidth of cooperation.**  
*Layer:* L2/L4 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Trust Dynamics  
> Trust không phải là một cảm xúc; nó là một computational resource cho coordination.

**M13: Fear is information, not instruction.**  
*Layer:* L2 | *Field:* F-3 | *Module:* Fear Systems  
> Fear báo hiệu threat; nó không bắt buộc phản ứng. Prefrontal có thể override amygdala.

**M14: Status is a field, not a position.**  
*Layer:* L13 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Status Hierarchies  
> Status không phải là một điểm trong hierarchy; nó là một distributed field.

**M15: Memes are cultural genes operating in social fields.**  
*Layer:* L5 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Cultural Memetics  
> Memes replicate, mutate, and select — giống genes nhưng trong cognitive/social field.

**M16: Executive function is the bottleneck of civilization.**  
*Layer:* L1/L3 | *Field:* F-3 | *Module:* Executive Psychology  
> Cognitive bottleneck limits everything. Prefrontal capacity is the scarcest resource.

**M17: Incentives shape behavior more than intentions.**  
*Layer:* L13 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Incentive Design  
> Don't ask what people want; ask what system incentivizes them to do.

**M18: Coalitions form around shared threat or shared meaning.**  
*Layer:* L4/L13 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Coalition Behavior  
> Alliance formation = field resonance. Shared threat (fear-based) or shared purpose (love-based).

**M19: Identity drifts when narrative control is externalized.**  
*Layer:* L4 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Organizational Identity  
> Identity = self-model + narrative. Khi narrative bị kiểm soát bởi external forces, identity drifts.

**M20: Conflict is a signal of model mismatch, not moral failure.**  
*Layer:* L3/L6 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Conflict Dynamics  
> Conflict = prediction error between two mental models. Giải quyết = model update, không phải victory.

### M21-M30: Thực Hành & Phục Hồi

**M21: Burnout is entropy accumulation, not weakness.**  
*Layer:* L8 | *Field:* F-2 | *Module:* Burnout Entropy  
> Burnout = system disorder khi energy input < entropy production. Không phải character flaw.

**M22: Elite cognition has blindspots proportional to its power.**  
*Layer:* L3/L6 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Elite Cognition  
> Power reduces feedback. Elite systems need external observability (L15) để correct.

**M23: Narrative control is sense-making warfare.**  
*Layer:* L5 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Narrative Control  
> Who controls the story controls the field. Narrative = field architecture of meaning.

**M24: Legitimacy is consent architecture, not authority.**  
*Layer:* L6/L13 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Legitimacy Systems  
> Authority without legitimacy = coercion. Legitimacy = perceived rightness of power.

**M25: Loyalty is bond coherence, not obligation.**  
*Layer:* L2/L4 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Loyalty Structures  
> Loyalty không phải là một contract; nó là một field state của mutual attachment.

**M26: Fragility is exposed when stress exceeds cross-layer coherence.**  
*Layer:* L1-L6 | *Field:* F-5-F-1 | *Module:* Human Fragility  
> Fragility không phải là một property; nó là một relationship giữa load và coherence.

**M27: Adaptation rate determines survival, not strength.**  
*Layer:* L7 | *Field:* F-2 | *Module:* Human Adaptation Loops  
> It's not the strongest that survives; it's the most adaptable. Learning rate > current knowledge.

**M28: Meta-awareness is the only layer that can observe all other layers.**  
*Layer:* L14/L15 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Meta-Awareness  
> Observability = prerequisite for adaptation. Không có self-observation, không có course correction.

**M29: Stress overload is not solved by removing stressors.**  
*Layer:* L8 | *Field:* F-3/F-1 | *Module:* Stress Overload  
> Solved by restoring cross-layer coherence faster than load accumulates.

**M30: Greed is existential anxiety converted to infinite acquisition.**  
*Layer:* L4/L5 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* Greed Architecture  
> The greedy system is not hungry; it is terrified of emptiness.

**M31: Anger is a boundary signal, not a weapon.**

> Qi không phải là một năng lượng huyền bí; nó là đo lường cross-layer field coherence.

**M33: Human systems intelligence is the capacity to read, map, and design across all layers.**  
*Layer:* L9-L14 | *Field:* F-1 | *Module:* All  
> Hiểu Nhân → Thành Vị = reading the full stack.

**M34: This architecture is a living document.**  
*Layer:* L14 | *Field:* F-5 | *Module:* Meta-Awareness
