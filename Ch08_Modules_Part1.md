## Chương 8: Human Systems — Motivation, Trust, Fear, Status, Memetics

---

### 8.1 Motivation Architecture

**Định nghĩa:** "Incentive stack & 3 gaps"

**OS Layers:** L4/L5  
**Field:** F-1 (social attention field)

**Core Stack:**
```
L4 Self-Model: "I am capable of achieving X"
    ↓
L13 Incentive: Reward prediction error (dopamine)
    ↓
L3 Mental Models: "Path A leads to reward B"
    ↓
L5 Spirit: "This reward matters to my purpose"
    ↓
L1 Brain: Striatal activation + prefrontal valuation
```

**3 Gaps:**
1. **Intention-Action Gap:** L3 biết nhưng L1 không execute
2. **Reward-Prediction Gap:** L13 dopamine phản ứng với prediction error, không phải reward itself
3. **Meaning-Performance Gap:** L5 purpose không align với L4 identity

### 8.2 Trust Dynamics

**Trust Equation (theo C. Mayer et al., adapted):**
```
Trust = (Ability × Benevolence × Integrity) / (Risk × Uncertainty)
```

**OS Layers:** L2/L4/L6  
**Repair Protocol:**
1. Acknowledge violation (L2 emotion naming)
2. Reconstruct predictability (L3 model update)
3. Re-establish benevolence (L4 self-model repair)
4. Verify integrity through action (L6 reality testing)

### 8.3 Fear Systems

**Fear Stack:**
```
L1 Amygdala: Threat detection (20ms response)
    ↓
L2 Emotion: Fear / Anxiety / Dread (graded response)
    ↓
L3 Mental Models: "This situation is dangerous"
    ↓
L4 Self-Model: "I am vulnerable / I will survive"
    ↓
L5 Spirit: Existential fear vs. integrated fear
    ↓
L6 Physics: Actual threat probability assessment
```

Theo Porges (Polyvagal Theory), fear response follows phylogenetic hierarchy:
1. Social Engagement (ventral vagus) — newest
2. Sympathetic Mobilization (fight/flight)
3. Parasympathetic Immobilization (dorsal vagus — shutdown)

(Porges, 1995, 2007, 2011)

### 8.4 Status Hierarchies

**3 Dimensions:**
1. **Dominance:** Coercive power (L13 threat-based)
2. **Prestige:** Earned respect (L13 skill-based)
3. **Virtue:** Moral standing (L5/L6 integrity-based)

**6 Mechanisms:**
- Attention capture, Deference signaling, Resource allocation, Coalition formation, Narrative control, Legitimacy construction

### 8.5 Cultural Memetics

**Meme Lifecycle:**
```
Inception (F-4 quantum potential) → Replication (F-1 social transmission)
→ Variation (L3 cognitive mutation) → Selection (L13 incentive filtering)
→ Retention (L4 identity integration) → Extinction (F-1 field decoherence)
```

Theo Dawkins (1976) và Blackmore (1999), memes là replicators văn hóa tương tự genes. Henrich (2016) chỉ ra rằng cultural evolution (dual inheritance theory) đã định hình psychology của populations WEIRD và non-WEIRD khác biệt sâu sắc. (Dawkins, 1976; Blackmore, 1999; Henrich, 2016)
